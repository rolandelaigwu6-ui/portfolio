ABOUT RT. HON. CHIEF DR. NELSON ALAPA, PhD

A life shaped by service and community

A leader, education advocate, philanthropist and community developer from Benue South.

Leadership in the community

BIOGRAPHY

Rooted in Benue South

Born in the mid-1960s to Chief Onyilokwu Alapa in Otukpo, Benue State, Dr. Alapa’s background connects the Idoma and Igede communities of Benue South. His work reflects a conviction that leadership should listen closely, act responsibly and create opportunity for people to thrive.

EDUCATION

Learning in the service of leadership

His published academic record includes schooling in Owerri and Makurdi; an ND in Criminology and Security Management; Advanced Diploma and BSc qualifications in Business Management; a PGD in Peace and Conflict Resolution; an MSc in Legislative Studies; and a PhD in Legislative Studies.

PUBLIC SERVICE

Experience that serves the public good

His record includes service in the House of Representatives, committee work spanning agriculture, petroleum downstream, electoral matters and the Nigeria Army, and membership of the University of Ibadan Governing Council. He continues to champion education, youth empowerment, social welfare and sustainable community development.

VISION

A stronger Benue South

His vision is a prosperous, secure and economically vibrant Benue South where people can access quality education, healthcare, infrastructure, employment opportunity and responsible leadership.
