# Image assets

Place approved, high-resolution images for the political leadership platform in this folder. The home page currently expects these names:

- `hero-governorate.jpg` — wide, authentic community/leadership photograph (recommended: 2200 × 1200 px)
- `project-infrastructure.jpg` — community, jobs, or enterprise image (1200 × 800 px)
- `project-education.jpg` — school or education image (1200 × 800 px)
- `project-health.jpg` — health, safety, or family wellbeing image (1200 × 800 px)

The detailed routes also use these images. Add only approved, original files with these exact names:

- `about-nelson-alapa.jpg`

- `news-field.jpg`

The Business route has a dedicated visual narrative. Add these original, approved images:

- `business-enterprise.jpg` — wide business-route hero, 2200 × 1100 px
- `business-market.jpg` — community/enterprise scene, 1600 × 1100 px
- `business-maritime.jpg` — maritime operations, 1200 × 800 px
- `business-real-estate.jpg` — real-estate development, 1200 × 800 px
- `business-farming.jpg` — farming or agriculture, 1200 × 800 px
- `business-people.jpg` — skills, mentorship or entrepreneurship, 1200 × 800 px
- `business-harbour.jpg` — wide maritime closing image, 1800 × 1000 px

Use official photography with documented permission and consent. Avoid identifying private citizens, particularly children, unless appropriate releases are in place. Images will automatically crop to fit their card areas.
